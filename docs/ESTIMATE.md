### Executive Architectural Guarantee

The central failure mode of previous AI lighting tools is architectural alteration: changing window counts, shifting roof pitches, mutating brick into stone, or erasing mature Texas landscaping. 

Our decoupled **Two-Stage Generative Pipeline** structurally eliminates this flaw:
1. **Stage 1 (Cleaned Dusk Property Master):** SAM-2 structural boundary vector mask locks the property geometry at 100% fidelity. Converts daytime daylight into a deep Texas blue-hour twilight, illuminates interior window apertures at 2700K architectural warm white, and semantically inpaints parked driveway vehicles and trash cans into clean pavement.
2. **Stage 2 (Vector Roofline Hardware Compositor):** Composites commercial C9 LED bulbs (15" spacing, 2/2 repeating patterns) and Omni Permanent downward wall-wash beams (8" spacing, 30° cones) strictly along verified roofline polylines. Decor elements (36"/48" wreaths, tree trunk & branch wraps, ground stakes) anchor to verified property coordinates.

General Managers (GMs) in their service trucks generate photorealistic 2K hero renderings and 2x2 inspiration sheets in under 1.5 seconds during the critical 10–13 minute in-home consultation window.
